# RentKA SEO Baseline - 30 July 2026

**Baseline date:** 30 July 2026  
**Search Console property:** `sc-domain:rentka.co`  
**Performance window:** 1–28 July 2026 (Google Search Console's latest complete 28-day window)  
**Purpose:** Permanent post-deployment benchmark for comparison on 30 August 2026

> This is an observation-only baseline. Rankings are approximate and reflect the signed-in Google session in Pakistan on 30 July 2026. Google results can vary by location, personalization, device, and time. No website or Search Console settings were changed.

## 1. Executive Summary

| Metric | Baseline |
|---|---:|
| Total clicks | 108 |
| Total impressions | 8,124 |
| Average CTR | 1.3% |
| Average position | 9.2 |

- **Clicks** are visits to RentKA from Google web-search results.
- **Impressions** count occasions when a RentKA result was shown in Google Search.
- **CTR** is clicks divided by impressions. The site received approximately 1.3 clicks per 100 impressions during the period.
- **Average position** is the impression-weighted average position of the highest RentKA result. A value of 9.2 places the average impression near the bottom of page 1, although individual queries vary substantially.

The baseline shows meaningful visibility from route-distance content, airport searches, Rawalpindi car-rental searches, and branded searches. Branded demand is the strongest click contributor, while several route queries already generate hundreds of impressions without clicks.
