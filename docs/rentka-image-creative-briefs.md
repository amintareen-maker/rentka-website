# RentKA Image Creative Briefs

## Production rules for every creative

- Generate the photographic base without logos, words, prices, phone numbers, badges, or watermarks.
- Add the official RentKA logo afterward from `public/logo.png`. Never trace, recreate, restyle, recolour, crop, or distort it.
- Pakistani right-hand-drive context: steering wheel on the right and traffic travelling on the left.
- Show a clean, stock-looking vehicle with natural proportions, intact wheels, plausible reflections, and no visible manufacturer or rental-company logo alterations.
- Use a neutral, realistic Pakistani registration plate that is fully blurred/unreadable, or frame the vehicle so the plate is not visible. Never invent a readable plate.
- Do not generate readable road signs, fake landmarks, foreign signs, yellow taxi livery, luxury-car styling, crowds, collages, or excessive graphic decoration.
- Use RentKA dark blue `#0F2B46` and green `#5BAE4A` only in subtle production overlays, gradients, rules, and CTA shapes—not as vehicle paint.
- Maintain WCAG-friendly contrast. Keep all important text inside a 72 px safe margin for square/portrait/landscape and a 96 px safe margin for stories.
- Overlay font: a clean geometric sans serif such as Montserrat, Inter, or Poppins. Headline: ExtraBold. Supporting copy: Medium/SemiBold.
- The only permitted phone number is `03020589999`.
